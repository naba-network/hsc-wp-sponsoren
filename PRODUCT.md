# Product

Sponsor page of SC Samina Hohenems (ice hockey club, Hohenems, Vorarlberg). Replaces the current page at sc-hohenems.at/sponsoren.

- **Mode:** Persuade (sponsors must feel valued) and Read (fans find a company fast).
- **Audiences:** fans/spectators, sponsors/partners (do they look good here?), club staff.
- **Language:** German only, informal "du".
- **Tiers:** Hauptsponsor (1, with text and button), Premium, Partner, Sponsoren, Gönner & Freunde.
- **Single source of truth:** every cell comes from a sponsor entry: name, logo, website, partner since, description, category. Shortcode `[hsc-sponsoren-grid kategorie= grid-items=]`.
- **SEO:** the sponsor name is real text below the logo.
- **Brand:** black `#1A1A1A` and light grey `#F5F5F7`, Bebas Neue (display), Roboto Condensed (text). Legacy blue/yellow is not used.
- **Assumptions (no interview held):** dark header with white logo as on the current site; fake sponsors only (public repo).
